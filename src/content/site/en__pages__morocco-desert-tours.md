## **Let’s Explore The Moroccan Desert!**

Taking a desert tour in Morocco that includes a camel ride across the dunes should be on everyone’s bucket list when visiting Morocco. Our desert tour packages feature camel trekking and at least one night of camping in the **[desert](https://www.britannica.com/place/Sahara-desert-Africa)**, where you can enjoy the Sahara’s breathtaking sunset and sunrise. We also offer additional activities such as sandboarding, ATV rides, and dune buggy excursions.

Join us on a multi-day tour into the desert, uncovering the nomad villages and hidden parts of the [Sahara desert](https://earth.google.com/web/c/ChE6DxIJL20vMDF3MXZ0GAEoAg). During our desert tours, you can stay in standard camps, spend time with a nomad family to experience their way of life or upgrade the camp to luxurious glamping. To **experience the true essence of the Moroccan desert**, with its rolling golden dunes, a visit to Erg Chebbi or Erg Chegaga is a must. These two deserts are known as the highest and largest sand dunes in Morocco.
