The desert around Erg Chebbi is characterized by wide open spaces, tall golden dunes, rocky tracks, and small oases hidden among the sands. When you drive your own vehicle, you are not only traveling through the desert, but also living a true adventure where every turn brings something new to discover. Unlike standard tours that follow the same fixed routes, a self-drive journey gives you the chance to explore places that many visitors never see. These lessons are very useful, especially for those who are new to desert conditions. The guide will show how to reduce tire pressure so the vehicle can move more easily over soft sand, how to drive safely on steep dunes, and what to do if the car gets stuck. Learning these simple but important techniques makes the journey safer and much more enjoyable.</p>

The sound of the tires moving over sand and gravel, the endless views of orange dunes on the horizon, and the feeling of freedom make the experience truly special. Driving your own 4×4 in Merzouga is not just about <a href="/morocco-rental-cars" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><b>transportation</b></a>; it is about becoming part of the desert itself.</p>'
