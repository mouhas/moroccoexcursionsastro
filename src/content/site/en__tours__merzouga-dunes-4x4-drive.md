The guide will show how to reduce tire pressure so the vehicle can move more easily over soft sand, how to drive safely on steep dunes, and what to do if the car gets stuck. Learning these simple but important techniques makes the journey safer and much more enjoyable.</p>

Driving your own 4×4 in Merzouga is not just about <a href="https://morocco-excursion.com/morocco-rental-cars/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><b>transportation</b></a>; it is about becoming part of the desert itself.</p>'

  aHtml: <p>While you don’t need to be a professional off-road driver, having some experience with driving on sand, gravel, or uneven terrain is highly recommended. The desert presents unique challenges, including soft dunes, rocky tracks, and sudden elevation changes, which can be difficult for first-time drivers. For beginners, a local guide is invaluable—they provide hands-on advice, demonstrate safe techniques, and help navigate tricky sections, ensuring a safer and more enjoyable experience.</p>
- q: What type of 4x4 is required?
  aHtml: <p>For a self-drive tour in the Merzouga desert, a robust 4x4 vehicle with low-range gearing is essential. Recommended models include the Toyota Land Cruiser, Mitsubishi, or similar SUVs capable of handling both soft sand dunes and rocky desert tracks.</p>
