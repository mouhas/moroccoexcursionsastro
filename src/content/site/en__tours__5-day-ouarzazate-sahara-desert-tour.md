faqs:
- q: How is this tour different from the shorter Ouarzazate desert tours?
  aHtml: <p>This 5-day tour takes the Draa Valley and Zagora route into the desert rather than the direct Dades route, and includes a full free day in Merzouga with two nights in the desert. It's a good fit if you want to see more of the south and spend more time among the dunes.</p>
- q: What accommodation is included?
  aHtml: <p>You'll stay one night in a hotel in Ouarzazate, one night in a hotel in Zagora, and two nights in a desert camp in Merzouga, all with dinner and breakfast included. Superior and luxury upgrades are available throughout.</p>
- q: What can I do on the free day in Merzouga?
  aHtml: <p>You can relax at the camp, walk in the dunes, or book an optional dune-buggy or quad-bike excursion across Erg Chebbi. A visit to a local nomad family is also a popular addition.</p>
- q: Is the camel trek suitable for everyone?
  aHtml: <p>The camel ride to the desert camp takes about an hour and suits most travelers. It isn't recommended for pregnant women, people with back problems, or those over 70; anyone who prefers not to ride can be driven directly to the camp instead.</p>
- q: What should I pack for this tour?
  aHtml: <p>Bring closed walking shoes, sunscreen, a hat or scarf, sunglasses, a flashlight, and a light jacket for cool desert nights, plus a warmer layer if traveling in winter.</p>
- q: Where does the tour start and end?
  aHtml: <p>The tour starts and ends in Ouarzazate, with pickup and drop-off at your hotel, riad, or another agreed location. It's a round trip, so you don't need separate transport arrangements at the end.</p>
- q: Is this a private tour or will we be grouped with other travelers?
  aHtml: <p>This is a private tour, so it's just your group and the driver-guide in the vehicle throughout, with no other travelers joining. The pace and stops can be adjusted to suit you.</p>
- q: What's the best time of year to do the Draa Valley and Zagora route?
  aHtml: <p>Spring and autumn are the most comfortable, with mild days for the Draa Valley drive and pleasant desert nights. Summer can be extremely hot around Zagora and Merzouga, and winter nights at the camp get cold, so a warm layer is essential.</p>
- q: Is there mobile signal in Zagora and at the Merzouga desert camp?
  aHtml: <p>Zagora has reasonable signal since it's a proper town. Once you're out on the desert tracks toward Merzouga and at the camp itself, coverage becomes patchy or disappears entirely, so treat the two nights in the dunes as a break from connectivity.</p>
- q: Can this tour be done with children?
  aHtml: <p>Yes, families are welcome. The camel trek and desert camp are generally enjoyable for children, though the driving days are long, so it suits families comfortable with several hours in the car between stops.</p>
