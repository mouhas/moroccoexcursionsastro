  html: '<p>After breakfast, you''ll head south along the Draa Valley, one of Morocco''s longest palm-lined valleys, passing kasbahs, small villages, and date plantations along the way. The road follows the Draa River as it winds through increasingly arid terrain toward Zagora.</p>
