  html: '<p>The camel trek in Merzouga starts around 16:00 during winter and around 18:00 during summer and hot months. These golden dunes are some of the highest in North Africa, and their color changes beautifully during the day.</p>

    <p>As you ride deeper into the desert, the guides will stop for photos and to let you enjoy the amazing views. One of the best moments is stopping on top of a dune to watch the sunset. The sand turns shades of orange and red as the sun goes down — a truly magical sight.</p>

    <p>When you arrive at the desert camp, you will be welcomed with a glass of mint tea. After settling into your tent, you can walk on the dunes, relax, or try sandboarding. In the evening, a tasty Moroccan dinner will be served in the restaurant tent, with dishes like tagine and couscous.</p>

    <p>After dinner, the camp comes alive with Berber music. The local guides will play drums around the campfire, and you can join them by drumming or dancing. At night, the sky is full of bright stars, perfect for stargazing and enjoying the quiet of the Sahara.</p>'
