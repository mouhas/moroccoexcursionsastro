- title: '1st day everning: Our office in Merzouga - Camel ride in the dunes - Desert Camp - Sunset'
  html: '<p>The camel trek in Merzouga starts around 16:00 during winter and around 18:00 during summer and hot months. We will meet at our office in Merzouga, where you will park your car, use the bathroom, get prepared for the tour. You will ride the camels across the Erg Chebbi dunes, the tallest and most famous dunes in Morocco. These golden dunes are some of the highest in North Africa, and their color changes beautifully during the day.</p>

    <p>As you ride deeper into the desert, the guides will stop for photos and to let you enjoy the amazing views. One of the best moments is stopping on top of a dune to watch the sunset. The sand turns shades of orange and red as the sun goes down — a truly magical sight.</p>

    <p>When you arrive at the desert camp, you will be welcomed with a glass of mint tea. After settling into your tent, you can walk on the dunes, relax, or try sandboarding. In the evening, a tasty Moroccan dinner will be served in the restaurant tent, with dishes like tagine and couscous.</p>

    <p>After dinner, the camp comes alive with Berber music. The local guides will play drums around the campfire, and you can join them by drumming or dancing. At night, the sky is full of bright stars, perfect for stargazing and enjoying the quiet of the Sahara.</p>'
- title: '2nd day morning: Return to Merzouga'
  html: '<p>On the second day, you will wake up early to see the sunrise. Climb a nearby dune and watch as the first light of day shines over the golden sand. It is one of the most beautiful sights in the desert.</p>

    <p>Afterwards, you will enjoy breakfast in the camp and take a shower before leaving. Then, you will ride the camels back through the dunes to Merzouga. The morning light gives the desert a different look, making the journey just as special as the evening before.</p>

    <p>By the end of the trip, you will have lived an unforgettable Sahara experience — with camel rides, sunsets, music, and the peaceful beauty of the desert.</p>'
