notes:
- Morocco is a very beautiful and photogenic country, rich in colorful landscapes, historic towns, and lively markets. If you would like to take photos of people, always ask their permission first. Most locals are happy to be photographed, but some may ask for a small tip. Being polite and respectful will help you enjoy your experience even more.
- Most hotels, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riad" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong>riads</strong></a>, and cafes in Morocco offer Wi-Fi, so you can stay connected during your trip. However, in remote areas like the Sahara Desert or small villages, the internet signal can be weak or nonexistent. It is a good idea to download maps or important trip information before heading to these places.
- It is important to have cash on hand when visiting small towns, markets, and desert camps, as many businesses do not accept credit or debit cards. ATMs are common in cities, but they are not always available in rural or desert areas. Make sure you have enough cash to cover meals, souvenirs, and small expenses during your trip.
- Moroccan cuisine is delicious, with many dishes made from fresh vegetables, meat, and traditional spices. Some dishes can be quite spicy, so if you don't like spicy food, you can ask for milder versions. Always drink bottled water to avoid stomach issues. If you have dietary restrictions or allergies, let your guide or restaurant staff know in advance so they can ensure there are suitable options for you.
- The climate in Morocco can vary greatly depending on the season and location. During the <a href="/morocco-desert-tours" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong>desert excursion</strong></a>, nights can be very cold, especially from November to February. Even though days are hot, the temperature can drop quickly after sunset. Bring warm clothes, a hat, and a good jacket to stay comfortable if you plan to sleep in a desert camp.
- Morocco is bigger than it looks on the map. The drive from Fes to Merzouga or from Merzouga to Ouarzazate can take between 6 and 9 hours, including breaks. Many excursions involve several hours of driving, so be prepared for long but beautiful drives through mountains, valleys, and deserts.
