In one trip, you get the High Atlas Mountains and the Sahara desert, the imperial cities of the north, the blue streets of Chefchaouen, and the Atlantic coast towns of Casablanca and Essaouira. itinerary:
- title: 'Day 1: Agadir - Marrakech'
  html: '<p>Your driver-guide will pick you up from your hotel or the airport in Agadir in the morning. From there, we head north toward Marrakech, passing through argan tree country and small farming towns along the way.</p>

    <p>You''ll arrive in Marrakech in the afternoon and check into your riad or hotel, with the evening free to rest or take a first stroll around the city.</p>'
- title: 'Day 2: Marrakech - Free Day'
  html: '<p>Today is left free for you to explore Marrakech at your own pace. Wander the souks of the Medina, visit the Bahia Palace or the Majorelle Garden, and end the day at Jemaa el-Fna square as it fills with food stalls, musicians, and storytellers.</p>

    <p>Your driver-guide remains available if you''d like a half-day city tour instead of exploring on your own.</p>'
- title: 'Day 3: Marrakech - Ait Ben Haddou - Ouarzazate - Dades Valley'
  html: '<p>We leave Marrakech in the morning and cross the High Atlas Mountains over the Tizi-n-Tichka pass, Morocco''s highest road pass, with sweeping views of the valleys below.</p>

    <p>We''ll stop at Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO World Heritage kasbah known for its appearances in films and TV series, before continuing to Ouarzazate and on through Skoura''s palm groves to the Dades Valley, where we spend the night.</p>'
- title: 'Day 4: Dades Valley - Todra Gorges - Merzouga'
  html: '<p>After breakfast, we drive to the Todra Gorges for a walk through one of Morocco''s most dramatic canyons, its rock walls narrowing to just a few meters apart in places.</p>

    <p>From there we continue toward Merzouga, arriving in the afternoon. As the sun starts to drop, you''ll set off on a camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes and spend the night in a desert camp, with dinner and Berber music around the campfire.</p>'
- title: 'Day 5: Merzouga - Midelt - Ifrane - Fes'
  html: '<p>We wake early for the sunrise over the dunes, then head back to Merzouga for breakfast and a shower before the long drive north.</p>

    <p>The road takes us through the Ziz Valley and the market town of Midelt, then up into the Middle Atlas through Ifrane, known for its cedar forests and Alpine-style architecture, before arriving in Fes in the evening.</p>'
- title: 'Day 6: Fes - Free Day'
  html: '<p>Today is yours to explore Fes at your own pace. The Fes el-Bali Medina is one of the largest car-free urban areas in the world, with narrow lanes leading to tanneries, madrasas, and craft workshops.</p>

    <p>A guided half-day tour of the Medina and tanneries can be arranged if you''d prefer not to navigate the maze of streets alone.</p>'
- title: 'Day 7: Fes - Meknes - Volubilis - Chefchaouen'
  html: '<p>We leave Fes and stop first in Meknes, one of Morocco''s four imperial cities, known for its monumental gates and quieter, less touristy medina.</p>

    <p>From there we continue to Volubilis, the best-preserved Roman ruins in Morocco, before heading into the Rif Mountains to Chefchaouen, the blue-painted town where we spend the night.</p>'
- title: 'Day 8: Chefchaouen - Rabat - Casablanca'
  html: '<p>After a morning walk through Chefchaouen''s blue-washed alleys, we head south to Rabat, Morocco''s capital, stopping at the Hassan Tower and the Kasbah of the Udayas.</p>

    <p>We continue to Casablanca in the afternoon, where you''ll have time to see the Hassan II Mosque from outside before checking into your hotel for the night.</p>'
- title: 'Day 9: Casablanca - Essaouira'
  html: '<p>This morning we drive south along the coast to Essaouira, a laid-back port town known for its ramparts, fishing harbor, and windswept beaches.</p>

    <p>The rest of the day is free to wander the medina, browse the woodwork and jewelry stalls, or relax by the water before your overnight stay.</p>'
- title: 'Day 10: Essaouira - Agadir'
