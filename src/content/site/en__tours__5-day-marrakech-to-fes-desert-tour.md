overviewHtml: '<p>This private 5-day desert tour to <a href="https://morocco-excursion.com/fes-tours/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><b>Fes</b></a> is a wonderful way to experience the natural beauty, culture, and history of Morocco. The journey begins in Marrakech and ends in Fes, passing through the High Atlas Mountains, Berber villages, ancient kasbahs, and the vast Sahara Desert. Along the way, travelers enjoy a mix of landscapes and activities, with overnight stays in the Dades Valley, the desert, and the mountain town of Midelt.</p>

  <p>The first part of the trip takes you through the winding roads of the High Atlas Mountains, offering incredible views and a chance to see traditional Berber life. One of the highlights of the day is visiting the famous Ksar of Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its beautiful mud-brick architecture and role in many films. The tour continues through the cinematic city of Ouarzazate, often called the “Hollywood of Africa,” and passes by lush oases and villages like Skoura and the Valley of Roses near Mgouna, before reaching the scenic Dades Valley for the first overnight stay.</p>

  <p>On the second day, the journey heads south toward the golden dunes of the <strong><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahara" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Sahara</a></strong>. Before arriving in the desert, there’s a stop at the spectacular Todra Gorges, a favorite place for hikers and nature lovers. After visiting the gorges and exploring the green palm groves nearby, travelers ride camels through the sand dunes to a desert camp. The night is spent under the stars, enjoying traditional food, music, and the peaceful silence of the desert.</p>

  <p>The third day is a full day to discover the Sahara Desert and its hidden treasures. Guests will visit nomadic families who still live in the desert, explore old abandoned villages, and stop at fossil sites that reveal the ancient history of the region. This day offers a deep cultural experience and a chance to see a different side of Morocco, far from the busy cities.</p>

  <p>As the tour continues on the fourth day, the route leaves the desert behind and travels through the long Ziz Valley and the town of <a href="https://morocco-excursion.com/errachidia-tours/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><b>Errachidia</b></a>. The drive brings you to Midelt, a peaceful town located between the Middle and High Atlas Mountains. It’s the perfect place to rest before the final stretch to Fes.</p>

  <p>On the last day, the road to Fes passes through scenic mountain areas. Highlights include a stop in Zaida, a small town known for its fresh mountain air, and the cedar forest near Azrou, where you might see monkeys in the wild. Before reaching Fes, the tour also passes through Ifrane, a town known for its clean streets, green spaces, and European-style houses — often called “Little Switzerland.”</p>

  <p>For your comfort, this tour offers two accommodation options: standard and luxury. Whether you prefer a simple stay or a more luxurious experience, you can choose the option that fits your style and budget. This private 5-day desert tour is perfect for those who want to explore Morocco’s most famous landscapes and cultural highlights in a short but unforgettable journey.</p>'

  html: '<p>On the first day, we will start the tour by picking you up from Marrakech. The drive will take us through small Berber villages and across the Tizi-n-Tichka pass in the High Atlas Mountains to Ait Benhaddou. This is a beautiful historic Kasbah and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Ait Benhaddou has been featured in many famous movies, such as <em>Lawrence of Arabia</em> and <em>Kingdom of Heaven</em>.</p>

    <p>After visiting the Kasbah, we will continue to Ouarzazate, known as the "Hollywood of Africa" because of its famous movie studios. Then, we will follow the Thousand Kasbahs road to Skoura, where we will explore the Kasbah of Amredil. Next, we’ll visit Kelaat M’gouna, the capital of the Valley of the Roses. Finally, we will arrive in Dades, where we will spend the night.</p>

    <p><strong>Accommodation:</strong><br/><em>Standard</em>: Hotel Kasbah Didis or a similar<br/><em>Superior</em>: Hotel Ksar Sultan or a similar</p>'

  html: '<p>The second day begins with a trip east to the golden dunes of Merzouga. The first stop is Tineghir, where you can enjoy stunning views of old mud villages and a large palm oasis stretching on both sides of the town. Next, the journey continues to Todra Gorges. Here, you can walk beneath tall red cliffs that rise up to 300 meters, carved by the Todra River.</p>

    <p>Afterward, the trip heads south through Jorf and Erfoud. Erfoud is famous as the "Fossil Capital" because many fossils have been found there. A short drive later, the vast sand dunes of Erg Chebbi appear on the horizon like a mirage.</p>

    <p>Upon arriving in Merzouga, you will check into your hotel/desert camp. Then, you have free time to walk in the nearby dunes and enjoy a beautiful Sahara sunset.</p>

- title: 'Day 3: Merzouga - Khamlia - Rocky Desert - Merdani - The Berber Nomads - Erg Chebbi Desert'
  html: '<p>The third day will be off-roading deeper into the desert to visit hidden and less-visited places. The first stop is Merzouga’s seasonal lake, but only if there is water during your visit. This magical lake offers amazing views, with tall golden dunes creating a beautiful background. When the lake has water, many birds come here, including graceful flamingos, making a peaceful and unique scene.</p>

    <p>Next, you will visit the small village of Khamlia. The people here originally come from Mali and Sudan. This village is famous for its rich culture. After walking around and seeing the village life, you will enjoy a visit to a local band that plays Gnaoua music. This is lively African percussion music full of rhythm and energy, perfect for dancing and feeling the local traditions.</p>

    <p>The adventure continues to the Black Desert, a huge volcanic rocky area east of Merzouga’s dunes (Erg Chebbi). This dark desert has many interesting minerals and fossils to see. You will have time to explore some abandoned villages scattered across the desert and stop at several viewpoints with wide views of the endless sand.</p>

    <p>Our next stop will be a large nomadic family that still lives a traditional life. They move from place to place with their animals. Here, you can learn about their daily life and customs. They might invite you to share mint tea or even lunch (optional) in their traditional tent or under the shade of a nearby acacia tree, giving you a chance to experience their world.</p>

    <p>Before going back to Merzouga, the safari will stop at a quiet desert oasis—a green spot with palm trees. Back in Merzouga, you will ride a camel deep into the desert to reach your camp for the night. The desert views will amaze you, especially the beautiful sunset over the golden dunes.</p>

    <p>At the camp, you will be welcomed with hot mint tea and shown to your tent to relax. After dinner, local musicians will play traditional drum music and invite you to try some rhythms. Before you go to sleep, take a calm walk on the nearby dunes and enjoy the amazing starry sky, far away from city lights.</p>

    <p><strong>Accommodation:</strong><br/><em>Standard</em>: Mouhou Standard Desert Camp or similar<br/><em>Superior</em>: Mouhou Luxury Desert Camp or similar</p>'

  html: '<p>The fourth day of the tour will be waking up early to watch a beautiful sunrise over the sand dunes. The rising sun creates amazing shadows and colors across the desert. It was once an important stop for caravan traders. Today, it has the largest market (souk) in the area, full of local goods and busy with lively activity.</p>

    <p>From Rissani, we will continue toward the Anti-Atlas Mountains. On the way, we will stop at several interesting places, including the beautiful Ziz Valley, known for its palm trees and river. We will also visit some small Berber villages where you can see traditional life and culture. Our route will take us through the Tizi Ntalghamt mountain pass, which offers amazing views of the surrounding landscape.</p>

    <p>Finally, we will arrive in the town of Midelt, where we will spend the night. Midelt sits between the Middle and High Atlas mountains. It is known for its fresh air and peaceful atmosphere, making it a perfect place to rest after a long day of travel.</p>
