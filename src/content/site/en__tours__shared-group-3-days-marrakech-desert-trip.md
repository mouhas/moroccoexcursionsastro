- q: How is the weather in the Sahara desert?
  aHtml: '<p>In <strong>summer</strong> (June to August), Merzouga gets really hot, over 100°F (38°C) during the day. Nights are warm too, around 68-77°F (20-25°C). It hardly rains, and the skies are clear.</p>

    <p><strong>Winter</strong> (December to February) brings milder days, around 50-68°F (10-20°C), good for exploring. But nights get cold, about 30-40°F (0-5°C) or even colder. Rain is rare, but sometimes there might be a little.</p>

    <p>This affects what you can do. Summer nights are great for activities, while winter days are more comfortable. Packing for both hot days and cold nights will make your desert trip in Merzouga better, no matter the season.</p>'
