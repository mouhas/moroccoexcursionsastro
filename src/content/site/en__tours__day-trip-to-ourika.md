- q: Which days does the Ourika Berber market run?
  aHtml: <p>The Tnin Ourika market runs on Mondays, Thursdays, and Fridays. If you visit on another day the stalls will mostly be closed, so let us know if the market is important to you and we can time your trip accordingly.</p>
- q: Is the hike to the Setti Fatma waterfalls difficult?
  aHtml: <p>The path to the first waterfall involves some rocky, uneven terrain and a bit of scrambling, but it's manageable for most reasonably fit travelers. Going further up to the higher waterfalls is steeper and better suited to those comfortable with a proper hike.</p>
- q: Do we need a separate local guide at Setti Fatma, and is that extra?
  aHtml: <p>A local guide leads the short hike to the first waterfall and isn't included in the base tour price, it's a small fee paid on the spot. Most travelers find it worthwhile since the paths aren't always clearly marked.</p>
- q: Is this a good trip to escape the summer heat in Marrakech?
  aHtml: <p>Yes, Ourika Valley sits higher in the Atlas foothills and stays noticeably cooler and greener than Marrakech, especially near the river and waterfalls, making it a popular summer escape.</p>
