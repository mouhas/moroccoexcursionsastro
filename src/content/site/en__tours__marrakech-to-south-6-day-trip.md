  html: '<p>The first day of the tour begins with a morning pick-up from your accommodation in Marrakech.<br/>Shortly after leaving the city, we’ll drive through the beautiful High Atlas Mountains and cross the famous <strong>Tizi N''Tichka Pass</strong> (2,260 meters above sea level). We’ll make a few stops along the way to take photos and enjoy the breathtaking views of the mountains, deep valleys, and small traditional Berber villages.</p>

    <p>Our next stop will be <strong>Kasbah Ait Ben Haddou</strong>, a UNESCO World Heritage site. This historic fortified village sits on a hill next to the green Ounila River. It is one of the most famous kasbahs in Morocco and has been the backdrop for many well-known movies and TV shows, such as <em>Lawrence of Arabia</em>, <em>The Mummy</em>, <em>Gladiator</em>, <em>Jesus of Nazareth</em>, and <em>Game of Thrones</em>.</p>

    <p>After exploring the kasbah, we will continue our journey to <strong>Dades Valley</strong>. On the way, we’ll pass through the <strong>Skoura Oasis</strong>, the <strong>Valley of Roses</strong> near Mgouna (famous for its rose products), and several traditional Berber villages.</p>

  html: '<p>Today we will travel from Dades Valley to the Sahara Desert.
