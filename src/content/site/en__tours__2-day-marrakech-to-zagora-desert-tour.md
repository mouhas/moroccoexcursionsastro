notes:
- The Zagora desert doesn’t have proper sand dunes; it is more of a rocky desert. While it is indeed a desert, it may not meet your dreamy expectations of high, orange sand dunes.
- This 2-day tour from Marrakech to Zagora is the shortest desert trip and includes a lot of driving. If you have enough time in Morocco and want to visit the real desert, it is better to <a href="https://morocco-excursion.com/contact/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><b>book</b></a> a 3- or <a href="/tours/4-day-marrakech-to-merzouga-desert-tour" rel="noopener" target="_blank">4-day tour to the Merzouga desert</a>, which has the highest dunes in Morocco.
- It’s possible to miss the sunset or parts of the camel trek, depending on the weather (e.g., rain or sandstorms).
- We generally stop at small, local restaurants that serve fresh food. However, if you prefer to choose or book a restaurant on your own, you can ask your driver to take you anywhere at any time.
- Riding a camel for a long time can be uncomfortable and is not recommended for pregnant women, people over 70 years old, or those with back problems.

- title: 'Day 1: Marrakech - Atlas Mountains - Kasbah Ait Ben Haddou - Draa Valley - Zagora Desert'
  html: '<p>On the first day of our two-day trip to the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagora,_Morocco" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Zagora desert</a>, we will pick you up from your accommodation, the bus station, or even Marrakech''s airport if you arrive early in the morning. After driving a few kilometers out of Marrakech, we will begin crossing the High Atlas Mountains, traveling through a winding road and the <span>Tizi n’Tichka pass</span> (2,260 meters above sea level). We will make several stops to take pictures and enjoy the impressive landscapes, panoramic views, and Berber villages of the Atlas.</p>

    <p>After crossing the Atlas Mountains, we will visit the <strong>UNESCO World Heritage Site</strong> of Ait Benhaddou Kasbah, recognized in 1987. Following our visit and lunch near the Kasbah, we will drive to the southern part of Morocco, crossing the second pass called Tizi N’Tnifift at 1,710 meters. We will then pass by Ait Saoun village, the former route of Sahara traders coming from Timbuktu.</p>

    <p>After a few kilometers, we will reach Agdez, the first administrative center that marks the source of the <strong>Draa Valley</strong> with its six successive palm groves and kasbahs. We will continue our desert adventure through the Draa Valley to reach Zagora. Upon arrival, we will ride camels waiting to take us on a short adventure. After watching the sunset and having some rest with mint tea, dinner will be served at the desert camp''s restaurant, followed by traditional drumming music.</p>

  aHtml: '<p>The weather in winter, from mid-November to early March, is characterized by cold nights and warm days, with temperatures around 0°C at night and 26°C during the day.</p>
