notes:
- When planning a Merzouga desert tour, it is important to know that some activities can be physically tiring. Riding camels, climbing tall dunes, or walking long distances in soft sand can take a lot of energy. Make sure you are ready for this level of activity and take breaks when you need to avoid getting too tired.
- The desert weather changes a lot. In summer, the daytime can be very hot, sometimes over 40°C (104°F). In winter, nights can be very cold, sometimes below 5°C (41°F). To stay comfortable, wear layers of clothing, use a hat or scarf to protect yourself from the sun, apply sunscreen, and bring warm clothes for the night. Drinking plenty of water is also very important because the desert air is dry.
- Safety in the desert is very important. Tours are led by experienced guides who know the desert well. Listening to your guide is very important for your safety, especially during <a href="https://morocco-excursion.com/morocco-camel-trekking/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong>camel rides</strong></a>, 4×4 or buggy rides, and overnight stays in the desert. Following instructions helps you enjoy your adventure safely.
- Also, remember that the desert tour plan may change because of the weather or other unexpected problems. Sandstorms, rain, or other conditions can affect activities. Being flexible and understanding these changes will help you enjoy your desert experience, even if the schedule is not the same as planned.

  html: '<p>The second day is about learning desert life. In the morning, you will visit nomad families to see how they live and work in the desert. You will see their tents, daily activities, and traditional crafts. Later, you will travel through Berber villages and meet local people.</p>

    <p>In the afternoon, you will visit Khamlia, a small village known for its Gnawa music. Here, you can listen to the music and enjoy the local culture. At the end of the day, you return to your desert camp for another magical sunset, dinner, and a night under a starry sky.</p>'

  html: '<p>Today is full of fun and adventure. In the morning, you will ride an ATV (quad bike) across the dunes for 2 hours. Later, you will have a break before switching to a buggy ride, which is faster and very exciting. During the day, you will stop at a desert oasis for lunch.</p>

    <p>The oasis is full of palm trees and fresh water, a calm place in the middle of the desert. After lunch, you return to your camp in Erg Chebbi for rest. In the evening, try sandboarding and enjoy dinner under the stars.</p>'
