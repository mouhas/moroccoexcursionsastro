    <p>Our first stop will be Khamlia, a small village known for its Gnawa people. They are descendants of Africans who came to Morocco more than a hundred years ago. The Gnawa have kept their culture alive through music and dance. In the village, you will visit a local association where they perform their traditional Gnawa music — a spiritual and energetic rhythm that reflects their history and heritage.</p>

    <p><strong>Mines &amp; Fossils</strong><br/>We will then continue to the M’ifis mines. Apart from being a tourist destination, the Merzouga desert is famous for its fossils and minerals. The mines are set on a high plateau that offers an incredible panoramic view of the Erg Chebbi dunes. These open mines are still active, and Berber workers face the daily challenge of working in pits more than 35 meters deep. Along the way, you may also come across fossil stones, for which this region is well known for.</p>

    <p><strong>Nomad Berber Families</strong><br/>During the tour, we will also visit a nomadic Berber family who still live in traditional tents in the middle of the Sahara.
