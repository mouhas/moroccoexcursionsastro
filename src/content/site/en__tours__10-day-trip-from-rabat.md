The final stretch follows the Atlantic coast to the fishing port of Essaouira before returning inland to Rabat.</p>


  <p>As a private, fully customizable tour, hotel categories, pace, and stops can all be adjusted to your budget and interests, whether you are traveling as a couple, a family, or a small group. This itinerary is designed to give first-time visitors a comprehensive introduction to Morocco''s diversity in a single ten-day loop.</p>'

- title: 'Day 1: Rabat'
  html: '<p>Your driver-guide will welcome you in Rabat, Morocco''s capital, and take you on a tour of the city''s main landmarks, including the unfinished Hassan Tower and the adjoining Mausoleum of Mohammed V, the Kasbah of the Udayas overlooking the Bou Regreg river, and the Royal Palace.</p>


    <p>You will spend the night in Rabat, resting before setting off on the road the next morning.</p>'
- title: 'Day 2: Rabat - Chefchaouen'
  html: '<p>Leaving Rabat, you will drive north through rolling countryside toward the Rif Mountains, arriving in Chefchaouen, the famous blue city, in the afternoon.</p>


    <p>The rest of the day is free to wander the old medina''s blue-washed lanes, browse the small shops, and enjoy the cool mountain air before an overnight stay in Chefchaouen.</p>'
- title: 'Day 3: Chefchaouen - Volubilis - Meknes - Fes'
  html: '<p>After breakfast, you will leave Chefchaouen and head toward Volubilis, Morocco''s best-preserved Roman ruins, where you can see ancient mosaics and columns still standing after nearly two thousand years.</p>


    <p>From there, you will stop in Meknes, one of Morocco''s four imperial cities, known for its monumental gates and the Bab Mansour. The day ends with the drive into Fes, where you will spend the next two nights.</p>'
- title: 'Day 4: Day to visit Fes'
  html: '<p>This full free day is set aside for exploring Fes el Bali, the largest car-free urban area in the world and home to the University of Al Quaraouiyine, one of the oldest in existence.</p>


    <p>With a local guide, you can wander the maze-like medina, visit the tanneries, madrasas, and souks, and see traditional artisans at work, before returning to your hotel for a second night in Fes.</p>'
- title: 'Day 5: Fes - Merzouga'
  html: '<p>Leaving Fes, the route climbs into the Middle Atlas through Ifrane, often compared to an alpine village, and the cedar forests of Azrou, home to wild Barbary macaques.</p>


    <p>Continuing south through Midelt and the Ziz Valley''s palm groves, you will reach Merzouga in the late afternoon in time for a camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes to watch the sunset, followed by dinner and music around the campfire at your desert camp.</p>'
- title: 'Day 6: Merzouga - Dades'
  html: '<p>After watching the sunrise over the dunes, you will return by camel or 4x4 to Merzouga for breakfast and a shower before setting off toward the Dades Valley.</p>


    <p>Along the way, you will pass through Erfoud and the Todra Gorges, one of Morocco''s most spectacular canyons, before continuing to the Dades Valley for the night, known for its dramatic rock formations and Berber kasbahs.</p>'
- title: 'Day 7: Dades - Marrakech'
  html: '<p>Leaving the Dades Valley, you will drive through the Valley of Roses at Mgouna and the palm groves of Skoura before reaching Ouarzazate.</p>


    <p>Along the way, you will stop at Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO World Heritage kasbah that has featured in numerous films, before crossing the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n''Tichka pass to arrive in Marrakech for the night.</p>'
- title: 'Day 8: Day to visit Marrakech'
  html: '<p>This free day is reserved for exploring Marrakech at your own pace. You can visit the bustling Jemaa el-Fna square, wander the souks of the medina, or relax in gardens such as the Majorelle Garden.</p>


    <p>Your driver-guide remains available to help arrange visits to any of the city''s monuments, such as the Bahia Palace or the Koutoubia Mosque, before a second night in Marrakech.</p>'
- title: 'Day 9: Marrakech - Essaouira'
  html: '<p>After breakfast, you will leave Marrakech and drive west across argan tree country toward the Atlantic coast, arriving in Essaouira by midday.</p>


    <p>The rest of the day is free to explore this relaxed fishing port, known for its fortified medina, seafood, and sea breeze, with an overnight stay in Essaouira.</p>'
- title: 'Day 10: Essaouira - Rabat'
  html: '<p>On this final day, you will leave the coast and drive back inland toward Rabat, passing through the countryside of the Chaouia and Doukkala regions.</p>


    <p>Your driver will drop you off at your accommodation, the airport, or the train station in Rabat in the afternoon or early evening, bringing the 10-day tour to a close.</p>'
