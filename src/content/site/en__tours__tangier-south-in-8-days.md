  <p>From Tangier, the route follows the coast through Assilah and Rabat to Casablanca and on to Marrakech. After a half day to enjoy the red city, the journey crosses the High Atlas Mountains, stopping at the UNESCO kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou on the way to Ouarzazate, the gateway to the south.</p>

  <p>After a half day in Fes, the final stretch takes you into the Rif Mountains to the blue city of Chefchaouen before returning to Tangier. As a private, fully customizable tour, hotel categories and pace can be adjusted to your preferences, making this an efficient way to connect the north of Morocco with its southern desert landscapes.</p>'
