faqs:
- q: What's included in the 2-day Ouarzazate to Merzouga tour?
  aHtml: <p>The price includes your private A/C vehicle, driver-guide, fuel, the camel trek, and one night in a desert camp with dinner and breakfast. Lunch and drinks along the way are not included.</p>
- q: Can I be dropped off somewhere other than Ouarzazate?
  aHtml: <p>Yes, this tour is often combined with onward travel to Fes or Marrakech. Let us know your plans when booking and we can arrange a drop-off at your next destination instead of returning to Ouarzazate.</p>
- q: What should I pack for this desert tour?
  aHtml: <p>Bring closed shoes, a scarf or hat, sunglasses, sunscreen, and a light jacket, since desert nights get cool even when the days are hot. A flashlight and a small backpack for the camel trek are also useful.</p>
- q: Is this tour suitable for everyone?
  aHtml: <p>The camel trek is not recommended for people with back problems, pregnant women, or travelers over 70, though a car transfer directly to the camp can be arranged instead.</p>
- q: What kind of accommodation is the desert camp?
  aHtml: <p>The standard camp includes shared bathroom facilities and comfortable tents with proper beds and bedding. Upgrades to more comfortable or luxury camps with private bathrooms are available on request.</p>
- q: What time does the tour start and end?
  aHtml: <p>Pickup in Ouarzazate is usually in the morning, and the tour ends the following morning after breakfast at the camp, once you've returned by camel or vehicle to meet your driver.</p>
- q: Should I tip the driver-guide?
  aHtml: <p>Tipping is optional but appreciated. For a two-day trip like this, around 5-10 euros per day for your driver-guide is a common amount.</p>
- q: When is the best time of year for this route?
  aHtml: <p>Spring (March-May) and autumn (September-November) offer the most comfortable temperatures for the Dades and Todra Gorges as well as the Merzouga dunes. Summer days in Merzouga can be very hot, while winter nights in the desert get cold.</p>
- q: Is there phone or wifi signal along the route?
  aHtml: <p>Signal is generally reliable in Ouarzazate, Skoura, and along most of the Dades and Todra Gorges. It becomes weaker once you reach the Merzouga desert camp, so it's best to let people know you may be briefly offline overnight.</p>
- q: What does the optional sandboarding involve?
  aHtml: <p>Sandboarding can be arranged at the Merzouga dunes near the desert camp, usually in the late afternoon or early morning. It's an easy add-on if you want extra time playing on the dunes beyond the camel trek.</p>
