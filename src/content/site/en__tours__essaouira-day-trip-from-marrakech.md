  aHtml: '<p>Essaouira is known as the “Windy City of Africa,” and it gets windy <strong>mostly from April to September</strong>.</p>

    <p>The <strong>strongest winds</strong> usually blow during the <strong>summer months</strong>—<strong>June, July, and August</strong>—when the coastal trade winds (called <strong>Alizé</strong>) are at their peak. These winds make Essaouira a top spot for <strong>kitesurfing and windsurfing</strong>, but they can feel quite strong for regular beachgoers.</p>

    <p>If you prefer a calmer visit, <strong>October to March</strong> tends to be less windy, with milder breezes and fewer tourists.</p>'
