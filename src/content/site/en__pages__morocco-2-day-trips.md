Spending two days in Morocco gives you a quick but exciting taste of the country’s culture, history, and natural beauty. Start in Marrakech, where the busy medina is full of narrow streets, colorful markets, and the smells of spices and street food. Jemaa el-Fnaa square is lively with street performers, storytellers, and food stalls, giving a real sense of Moroccan life.

On the second day, you can explore the beautiful landscapes outside the city. The Atlas Mountains offer stunning views, small Berber villages, and a chance to see local traditions up close. If you have less time, a short trip to the Agafay Desert or a camel ride near Marrakech gives a small taste of the Sahara, with peaceful sand dunes and amazing scenery.

Even in just two days, you can see Morocco’s mix of old and new, from historic palaces and gardens to modern cafés and shops. The friendly people, rich traditions, and unique sights make every moment special. These two days leave a strong impression and make visitors want to come back and explore more of this beautiful country.

## **2 Days in Marrakech**

**Day 1 – The Red City’s Treasures**

- Explore **Jemaa el-Fnaa Square** and **Marrakech Medina**.
- Visit **Bahia Palace**, **Saadian Tombs**, and **Koutoubia Mosque**.
- Relax in the **Majorelle Garden** & **YSL Museum**.
- Evening rooftop dinner overlooking Jemaa el-Fnaa.

**Day 2 – Choose Your Adventure**

- **Agafay Desert:** Camel ride, quad biking, desert lunch.
- **Atlas Mountains:** Trekking in Imlil, Berber villages, waterfalls.
- **Essaouira:** Coastal escape with seafood, beaches, and medina.
- **Ouzoud Waterfalls:** Hike, boat rides, and nature.

## **2 Days in Fes**

**Day 1 – Explore the Ancient Medina**

- Wander through the **Fes el-Bali Medina** (UNESCO World Heritage Site).
- Visit the **Bou Inania Madrasa**, **Al-Attarine Madrasa**, and **Kairaouine Mosque**.
- See the colorful **Chouara Tannery**.
- Shop for leather goods, carpets, and pottery in the souks.
- Evening: Traditional dinner with live Andalusian music.

**Day 2 – Day Trips Around Fes**

- **Volubilis & Meknes:** Roman ruins at Volubilis + imperial Meknes city.
- **Chefchaouen (Blue City):** 3–4 hours away, but worth a full-day trip to wander the painted blue streets.
- **Middle Atlas Mountains:** Explore Ifrane (the “Little Switzerland” of Morocco), Azrou cedar forest (with Barbary apes), and lakes.

## **2 Days in Tangier**

**Day 1 – Tangier City Highlights**

- Visit the **Kasbah Museum** and **Tangier Medina**.
- Stroll along the **Grand Socco** and the **Petit Socco**.
- See the **Hercules Caves** and **Cape Spartel**, where the Atlantic meets the Mediterranean.
- Evening: Enjoy seafood by the port or a café overlooking the bay.

**Day 2 – Excursions from Tangier**

- **Asilah:** A charming white-and-blue coastal town with art-filled medina.
- **Chefchaouen:** 2.5 hours away; ideal if you want a taste of the Blue City.
- **Tetouan:** Known for Andalusian architecture and authentic medina.
- **Beaches:** Spend time at Achakar or Dalia beaches for sun and relaxation.
