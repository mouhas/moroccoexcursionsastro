From the lively medina of **Marrakech** to the calm, blue streets of Chefchaouen, Morocco is full of unique experiences. In 12 days, you can explore ancient cities like **Fes**, with its old buildings and traditional tanneries, or visit the impressive UNESCO-listed Ait Ben Haddou, a historic ksar seen in famous films.

Adventure lovers can hike in the Atlas Mountains, explore the Todra and Dades Gorges, or ride camels across the golden sand dunes of the Sahara Desert, spending nights under the stars. Morocco’s coastal towns, like Essaouira, offer peaceful beaches, fresh seafood, and charming old streets.

Be sure to taste authentic Moroccan food, from flavorful tagines to refreshing mint tea, and enjoy a traditional hammam for relaxation. To experience local culture, attend festivals that celebrate music, crafts, and Berber traditions. These unforgettable 12 days will let you experience Morocco’s variety, making it a place where every traveler can enjoy history, adventure, and vibrant local life.

## What are the best things to do in Morocco in 12 days?

Here are 12 experiences that should not be missed when visiting Morocco. You can do all those experiences during a 12-day tour of Morocco.

## 1. Marrakech’s Medina

Step into the heart of Marrakech’s old town and get lost among its narrow streets. Explore bustling markets selling spices, textiles, and handmade crafts. Don’t miss the lively Jemaa el-Fnaa square, where street performers, food stalls, and storytellers create a magical atmosphere.

## 2. Explore Chefchaouen

Chefchaouen is a small city famous for its blue-painted streets and relaxed vibe. It’s perfect for strolling, taking photos, and shopping for local crafts. Surrounded by the Rif Mountains, the town also offers beautiful hiking opportunities nearby.

## 3. Adventure in the Sahara Desert

Visiting Morocco’s desert is a once-in-a-lifetime experience. Ride camels across the golden sand dunes, enjoy a sunset view over the Sahara, and spend the night in a traditional Berber camp beneath a star-filled sky.

## 4. The Atlas Mountains

The Atlas Mountains are a paradise for nature lovers and hikers. Discover scenic valleys, waterfalls, and small villages inhabited by Berber communities. Whether on a short day hike or a multi-day trek, the landscapes are unforgettable.

## 5. The City of Fes

Fes is a city steeped in history and culture. Explore the medieval medina, visit traditional tanneries, and marvel at centuries-old architecture. Fes is also home to the world’s oldest university, offering a glimpse into Morocco’s rich educational heritage.

## 6. The Coastal Charm of Essaouira

Essaouira is a charming town on Morocco’s Atlantic coast. Relax on sandy beaches, enjoy fresh seafood at the port, or explore the historic fortress walls and art galleries. The town’s relaxed pace makes it a perfect escape from busy city life.

## 7. Moroccan Cuisine

Moroccan food is a feast for the senses. Taste dishes like tagine, couscous, and pastilla, and don’t forget to try the famous Moroccan mint tea. Food in Morocco is a reflection of its culture, blending flavors from the Arab, Berber, and Mediterranean traditions.

## 8. Ait Ben Haddou

Ait Ben Haddou is a striking UNESCO-listed ksar, known for its traditional earthen buildings. Walk through the narrow alleys and imagine life centuries ago. The site has also been featured in famous movies, adding to its unique appeal.

## 9. Todra and Dades Gorges

These dramatic gorges are ideal for adventure seekers and photographers. Towering cliffs, winding roads, and small Berber villages create an unforgettable landscape. Hiking and rock climbing here are popular ways to enjoy the natural beauty.

## 10. Experience a Moroccan Hammam

A visit to a hammam is more than just relaxation—it’s a cultural ritual. Steam, scrub, and massage in a traditional bathhouse to experience Moroccan wellness practices that have been followed for centuries.

## 11. Discover Casablanca

Casablanca combines modernity with Moroccan tradition. Visit the stunning Hassan II Mosque, walk along the Corniche, and enjoy the city’s vibrant cafes and nightlife. It’s a city where the past and present come together beautifully.

## 12. Local Festivals

Morocco’s festivals are a celebration of culture, music, and traditions. Attend the Rose Festival in Kelaat M’Gouna or music events in Essaouira to immerse yourself in Moroccan customs and local life.

These 12 experiences capture the spirit of the country and ensure a memorable visit.
