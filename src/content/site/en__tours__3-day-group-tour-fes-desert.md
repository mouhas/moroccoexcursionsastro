- q: Where does the tour depart from in Fes?
  aHtml: <p>Pickup is arranged from your riad or hotel in Fes on the morning of departure, and the group is dropped back at the same location at the end of day three.</p>
- q: Can this be booked as a private tour instead of a shared group tour?
  aHtml: <p>Yes, the same Fes to Merzouga route can be arranged as a private tour with your own vehicle and driver-guide for an additional cost. Contact us if you'd prefer not to share the trip with other travelers.</p>
- q: What exactly is included and excluded on this tour?
  aHtml: <p>The price covers shared transport with air conditioning, fuel, an experienced driver, the camel trek in the dunes, sandboarding, and the desert camp with dinner and breakfast. Drinks and lunches along the way are not included.</p>
- q: How physically demanding is this tour?
  aHtml: <p>The tour itself is not physically demanding, with a moderate camel trek into the dunes and an easy walk in the cedar forest near Ifrane. The off-roading and nomad family visit on day two are done by 4x4, so no special fitness is required.</p>
- q: What is the best time of year to do this route?
  aHtml: <p>Spring and autumn offer the most comfortable temperatures for both the Middle Atlas passes near Ifrane and the Merzouga dunes. Summer can be very hot in the desert, while winter brings snow to the Middle Atlas and cold nights in Merzouga.</p>
- q: What should I pack for this trip?
  aHtml: <p>Bring warm layers for the Middle Atlas and desert nights, comfortable shoes for the camel trek and the short walk in the cedar forest, plus sunscreen, a hat, and a scarf for the dunes.</p>
- q: What type of accommodation is provided in the desert?
  aHtml: <p>You'll stay in a shared-style desert camp with proper tents, beds, and bedding, with dinner and breakfast included at the camp restaurant.</p>
- q: Should I tip the driver on this group tour?
  aHtml: <p>Tipping is optional but appreciated, especially since the driver is also your guide throughout the Middle Atlas and desert sections. A few euros per day from each traveler in the group is a common approach.</p>
