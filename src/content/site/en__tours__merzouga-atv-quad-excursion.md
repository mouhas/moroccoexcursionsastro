notes:
- When riding a quad bike in the desert, safety should always be your top priority. Always wear a helmet to protect your head, goggles to shield your eyes, and closed shoes for proper grip and protection. Sunglasses and a scarf are also very useful, as they help keep sand and dust out of your eyes, nose, and mouth. Even if you are planning just a short one-hour ride, taking these precautions will make your experience safer and more comfortable.
- Your local guide is not only there to lead the way but also to keep you safe. They know the desert routes, the tricky dunes, and the best paths better than anyone else. Always follow their instructions regarding speed, direction, and where to stop. This will help you avoid accidents, prevent the quad from getting stuck in soft sand, and ensure you get the most out of your ride.
- If it’s your first time riding an ATV, it’s a good idea to start small with a 1- or 2-hour ride. This will give you enough time to get used to the controls and feel comfortable driving on sand. Once you are confident, you can <a href="https://morocco-excursion.com/contact/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><b>book</b></a> a half-day or full-day adventure. Longer tours often include exciting stops, such as visiting traditional Berber villages, exploring hidden oases, or reaching panoramic viewpoints for amazing desert views.
- Riding an ATV is not very hard, but it does require effort. Driving through soft sand takes strength and focus, and you may feel tired after a few hours. The ride can also be bumpy at times, especially over uneven dunes. If you have back or neck problems, it’s recommended to choose a shorter ride for your comfort.
- We offer flexible options to suit your preference. You can ride alone on a quad for a fully independent adventure, or you can share one with another person. Riding double is a great choice for couples, friends, or <a href="https://morocco-excursion.com/morocco-family-tours/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><b>families</b></a> who want to enjoy the experience together.
