itinerary:
- title: 'Day 1: Ouarzazate - Skoura - Dades Valley'
  html: '<p>Your driver-guide will meet you in Ouarzazate in the morning and set off east toward the Dades Valley. Along the way, you will stop in Skoura, an expansive palm oasis dotted with earthen kasbahs and Berber villages, and, depending on the season, pass through the Valley of Roses around El Kelaa M''Gouna.</p>

    <p>The road then climbs into the Dades Gorges, where red rock formations known as the ''monkey fingers'' rise above the valley floor. You will spend the night in a hotel in the Dades Valley, with dinner and breakfast included.</p>'
- title: 'Day 2: Dades - Todra Gorges - Merzouga'
  html: '<p>After breakfast, you''ll leave Dades and drive to the Todra Gorges, one of Morocco''s most striking canyons, where sheer rock walls rise up to 300 meters on either side of a narrow river. You''ll have time for a short walk through the gorge before continuing toward Erfoud and on to Merzouga.</p>

    <p>Late in the afternoon, you''ll trade your vehicle for a camel and ride out across the Erg Chebbi dunes as the sun sets over the Sahara. Dinner and the night are spent at a desert camp among the dunes, with Berber music around the campfire under a sky full of stars.</p>'
- title: 'Day 3: Merzouga - Erfoud - Midelt - Ifrane'
  html: '<p>Rise early for sunrise over the dunes, then ride back to Merzouga for breakfast and a shower before your driver picks you up. The route heads north through Erfoud and the Ziz Valley, a long ribbon of palm groves squeezed between dry mountains.</p>

    <p>You''ll continue climbing into the Middle Atlas, passing through Midelt and its surrounding orchards, then through cedar forests near Azrou where Barbary macaques are often seen along the roadside. You''ll overnight in or near Ifrane, a small mountain town known for its alpine-style architecture, before the final push to Fes the next morning.</p>'
- title: 'Day 4: Ifrane - Arrive Fes - Guided City Tour'
  html: '<p>After breakfast, you''ll make the short drive to Fes, one of the world''s oldest continuously inhabited medieval cities. Once you arrive, a local guide will lead you on foot through the winding lanes of the Fes el-Bali medina, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the largest car-free urban areas on earth.</p>

    <p>You''ll see the famous Chouara tanneries, historic madrasas, and bustling souks selling leather, ceramics, and spices. The tour ends in Fes, where your driver-guide will drop you at your hotel or another location of your choice.</p>'

- q: Is this tour one-way from Ouarzazate to Fes?
  aHtml: <p>Yes, this is a one-way itinerary. Your driver-guide picks you up in Ouarzazate and the tour ends in Fes, so there is no need to backtrack. It's a great option if you're heading north to Fes after visiting Marrakech, Essaouira, or the south of Morocco.</p>
- q: What accommodation is used during the tour?
  aHtml: <p>You'll stay in a standard hotel in the Dades Valley, a desert camp in Merzouga, and a hotel near Ifrane, all with dinner and breakfast included. Superior and luxury upgrades are available for the hotels and the desert camp at an additional cost.</p>
- q: What should I pack for this tour?
  aHtml: <p>Bring comfortable closed shoes for walking in the gorges and the dunes, a hat or scarf, sunscreen, sunglasses, a flashlight, and a light jacket for cool desert nights. In winter, a warmer jacket and gloves are recommended, since temperatures in the Middle Atlas and the desert at night can drop significantly.</p>
- q: Is the camel trek suitable for everyone?
  aHtml: <p>The camel trek to the desert camp lasts about an hour and is generally comfortable for most travelers. It is not recommended for pregnant women, people with back problems, or those over 70, and travelers who prefer not to ride can be taken directly to the camp by 4x4 instead.</p>
- q: Can the itinerary be customized?
  aHtml: <p>Yes, stops, pace, and accommodation level can all be adjusted to fit your interests and schedule. Popular add-ons include a 4x4 excursion in the dunes, extra time in the Todra Gorges, or an additional night along the route.</p>
- q: Where does the tour start and end exactly?
  aHtml: <p>The tour starts with a pickup from your hotel, riad, or the bus/train station in Ouarzazate, and ends with drop-off at your accommodation in Fes after the guided medina tour. Airport transfers in either city can be arranged on request.</p>
