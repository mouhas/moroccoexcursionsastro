- q: What is the itinerary of this 3-day tour from Marrakech to Fes?
  aHtml: '<p>Day 1 crosses the High Atlas Mountains from Marrakech to the Kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou, then continues to Ouarzazate and the Dades Valley for the night. Day 2 travels through the Todra Gorges and Erfoud to reach the Merzouga desert and the Erg Chebbi dunes for a camel ride and a night in a desert camp. On the last day, the route heads north through the Ziz Valley, Midelt, the cedar forest of Azrou, and Ifrane before arriving in Fes.</p>'
- q: Does the tour end in Fes, or do I need to return to Marrakech?
  aHtml: <p>This tour is one-way and ends in Fes, so you do not travel back to Marrakech. It is a great option if you plan to continue your trip to Fes or fly out from there.</p>
- q: How does the camel ride in Merzouga work?
  aHtml: <p>On the second day, after arriving at Erg Chebbi, you will ride a camel across the dunes to reach the desert camp where you will spend the night. The ride takes place around sunset, so you will get to watch the colors of the dunes change as you travel.</p>
- q: What accommodation is provided during the tour?
  aHtml: <p>You will stay in a private room with a private bathroom at a hotel in the Dades Valley on the first night, and in a private tent at a desert camp near Merzouga on the second night, both with dinner and breakfast included.</p>
- q: What is the cancellation policy?
  aHtml: <p>You can cancel this tour free of charge up to 48 hours before the start date. Please get in touch as soon as possible if your plans change.</p>
- q: Will I see the Azrou cedar forest and its monkeys?
  aHtml: <p>Yes, on the final day the route passes through the cedar forest near Azrou, which is home to wild Barbary macaques that are often seen close to the road. This stop comes shortly before you reach the alpine-style village of Ifrane on the way to Fes.</p>
- q: What should I pack for this trip?
  aHtml: <p>Comfortable walking shoes for the Todra Gorges, a hat and sunscreen for the desert, and a warm layer for the cold desert night and the cooler temperatures around Ifrane and Azrou are all recommended.</p>
- q: Is this tour suitable for children or elderly travelers?
