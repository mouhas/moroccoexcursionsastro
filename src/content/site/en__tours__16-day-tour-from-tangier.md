itinerary:
- title: 'Day 1: Arrival in Tangier'
  html: <p>You'll be met on arrival in Tangier and transferred to your hotel. The rest of the day is free to explore the Kasbah, the Medina, or the seafront on your own.</p>
- title: 'Day 2: Tangier - Assilah - Rabat'
  html: <p>We leave Tangier and stop in Assilah, a small coastal town known for its whitewashed walls and murals, before continuing to Rabat, where we visit the Hassan Tower and the Kasbah of the Udayas and spend the night.</p>
- title: 'Day 3: Rabat - Casablanca'
  html: <p>After breakfast, we drive to Casablanca for a visit to the Hassan II Mosque, one of the largest mosques in the world, and some time along the Corniche before checking into your hotel.</p>
- title: 'Day 4: Casablanca - Essaouira'
  html: <p>We continue south along the coast to Essaouira, a relaxed port town with a fortified medina and long sandy beaches, where we check in for two nights.</p>
- title: 'Day 5: Essaouira - Free Day'
  html: <p>A full free day to explore Essaouira at your own pace—wander the ramparts, browse the woodwork stalls, watch the fishing boats come in, or simply relax by the water.</p>
- title: 'Day 6: Essaouira - Agadir'
  html: <p>We drive further south along the coast to Agadir, Morocco's main beach resort city, with time to relax by the ocean before continuing inland the next day.</p>
- title: 'Day 7: Agadir - Taroudant - Marrakech'
  html: <p>We head inland through Taroudant, sometimes called "Grandmother of Marrakech" for its ochre ramparts, before continuing north to Marrakech, where we arrive in the evening.</p>
- title: 'Day 8: Marrakech - Free Day'
  html: <p>Today is free to explore Marrakech—the souks, the Bahia Palace, the Majorelle Garden, and the evening buzz of Jemaa el-Fna square—at your own pace, with a guided tour available if you prefer.</p>
- title: 'Day 9: Marrakech - Ait Ben Haddou - Ouarzazate'
  html: <p>We cross the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi-n-Tichka pass, stopping at the UNESCO-listed Ait Ben Haddou kasbah, before arriving in Ouarzazate for the night.</p>
- title: 'Day 10: Ouarzazate - Dades Valley - Todra Gorges'
  html: <p>We drive through Skoura's palm groves and the Valley of Roses at Mgouna to the Dades Valley, then on to the Todra Gorges for a walk through the narrow canyon before our overnight stop.</p>
- title: 'Day 11: Todra Gorges - Merzouga'
  html: <p>We continue toward Merzouga through Berber villages and the town of Erfoud. In the late afternoon, you'll ride a camel into the Erg Chebbi dunes to watch the sunset, followed by dinner and music at the desert camp.</p>
- title: 'Day 12: Merzouga - Free Day'
  html: <p>After sunrise over the dunes, today is free to enjoy Merzouga at a relaxed pace, with options like a 4x4 dunes excursion or a visit to a nomad family, and a second night in the desert camp.</p>
- title: 'Day 13: Merzouga - Midelt - Ifrane - Fes'
  html: <p>We head north through the Ziz Valley, the market town of Midelt, and the cedar forests around Ifrane, arriving in Fes in the evening.</p>
- title: 'Day 14: Fes - Free Day'
  html: <p>A full free day in Fes to explore the Medina, the tanneries, and the madrasas, either on your own or with a guided half-day tour.</p>
- title: 'Day 15: Fes - Meknes - Volubilis - Chefchaouen'
  html: <p>We stop in Meknes and visit the Roman ruins at Volubilis before continuing into the Rif Mountains to Chefchaouen, the blue-painted town, for our final overnight stop.</p>
- title: 'Day 16: Chefchaouen - Tangier'
  html: <p>After a last morning walk through Chefchaouen's blue alleys, we drive back to Tangier, where you'll be dropped off at your hotel, the airport, or the ferry port, ending the 16-day tour.</p>
