- title: 'Day 1: Ouarzazate - Skoura - Dades Valley'
  html: '<p>Your driver-guide will meet you in Ouarzazate in the morning and head east toward the Dades Valley, stopping first in Skoura, a large palm oasis scattered with old kasbahs and Berber villages. Depending on the season, you may also pass through the Valley of Roses around El Kelaa M''Gouna.</p>

    <p>The road continues into the Dades Gorges, known for their striking red rock formations, where you''ll spend the night in a hotel with dinner and breakfast included.</p>'
- title: 'Day 2: Dades - Todra Gorges - Merzouga'
  html: '<p>After breakfast, you''ll drive to the Todra Gorges, one of Morocco''s most impressive canyons, with sheer walls rising up to 300 meters on either side of a narrow river. You''ll have time for a walk through the gorge before continuing through Erfoud toward Merzouga.</p>

    <p>Late in the afternoon, you''ll set off by camel into the Erg Chebbi dunes to watch the sunset. Dinner and the night are spent at a desert camp among the dunes, with music around the campfire under a sky full of stars.</p>'
- title: 'Day 3: Full Day in Merzouga'
  html: '<p>Today is spent entirely in and around the dunes, with no long drives. You can relax at the camp, walk among the dunes, or add on an optional 4x4 or quad-bike excursion to explore Erg Chebbi more widely.</p>

  html: '<p>After a final sunrise over the dunes and breakfast at the camp, you''ll ride back to Merzouga to meet your driver for the return journey. The route retraces the outbound road through Erfoud, the Todra Gorges, and the Dades Valley, with a stop for lunch along the way.</p>

    <p>You''ll arrive back in Ouarzazate in the evening, where your driver-guide will drop you at your hotel or another location of your choice.</p>'

faqs:
- q: Why choose this 4-day tour over the shorter 3-day version?
  aHtml: <p>This tour adds a full free day in Merzouga, giving you two nights in the desert instead of one. It's a better fit if you want time to properly enjoy the dunes, add an activity like a 4x4 excursion, or simply relax rather than turning straight around after one night.</p>
- q: What can I do on the free day in Merzouga?
  aHtml: <p>You can relax at the camp, explore the dunes on foot, or book an optional 4x4 or quad-bike excursion across Erg Chebbi. A visit to a nomad family living near the dunes is also a popular way to spend part of the day.</p>
- q: What accommodation is included?
  aHtml: <p>You'll spend one night in a hotel in the Dades Valley and two nights in a desert camp in Merzouga, all with dinner and breakfast included. Superior and luxury upgrades are available for both the hotel and the camp.</p>
- q: Is the camel trek suitable for everyone?
  aHtml: <p>The camel ride to the desert camp lasts about an hour and is comfortable for most travelers. It isn't recommended for pregnant women, people with back problems, or those over 70, and anyone who prefers not to ride can be driven directly to the camp instead.</p>
- q: What should I pack for this tour?
  aHtml: <p>Bring closed walking shoes, sunscreen, a hat or scarf, sunglasses, a flashlight, and a light jacket for cool desert nights. In winter, warmer layers are recommended for the evenings in the desert.</p>
- q: Does the tour return to Ouarzazate the same way it went out?
  aHtml: <p>Yes, this is a round trip, so the return journey on day 4 follows the same road back through the Todra Gorges and Dades Valley. We can adjust the stops on the way back so you see or revisit different spots than on the way out.</p>
- q: Is this a private tour or will we share the vehicle with others?
  aHtml: <p>The car and driver-guide are private to your group for all four days, from the pickup in Ouarzazate to the drop-off at the end. You can adjust the pace and stops as you go.</p>
- q: When is the best time of year for this route?
  aHtml: <p>Spring and autumn bring the most pleasant temperatures for the free day exploring Erg Chebbi and for walking through the Todra Gorges. Summer afternoons in Merzouga are very hot, while desert camp nights in winter can be cold.</p>
- q: Will we have phone signal in Merzouga and along the way?
  aHtml: <p>Ouarzazate, Skoura, and the Dades and Todra areas generally have reasonable signal. It becomes weaker once you're out among the Erg Chebbi dunes and can disappear entirely at the desert camp itself.</p>
- q: Should we tip the driver-guide?
  aHtml: <p>A tip isn't required but is customary for good service, especially given the extra day this tour spends in Merzouga compared to the shorter version. Some travelers also leave something for the camp staff or a nomad family they visit.</p>
