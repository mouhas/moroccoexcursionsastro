overviewHtml: '<p>This 10-day tour of Morocco departing from Casablanca is a journey through the country''s most iconic cities, its historic sites, and its breathtaking landscapes. Starting in Casablanca and ending in Marrakech, this trip blends culture, history, and adventure for a complete Moroccan experience.</p>

  <p>The tour begins in Casablanca, where travelers can explore the magnificent Hassan II Mosque before heading to Rabat, the capital of Morocco. From there, the road winds through the Rif Mountains to reach Chefchaouen, the famous "Blue Pearl" with its charming streets painted in blue.</p>

  <p>History lovers will enjoy visiting the Roman ruins of Volubilis and the imperial city of Meknes before continuing on to Fes, the cultural heart of the country. In Fes, the highlights include the medieval medina, the traditional tanneries, and the oldest university in the world.</p>

  <p>The journey then continues into nature, passing through the Middle Atlas Mountains, the cedar forests of Azrou, and the scenic Ziz Valley before arriving in Merzouga, on the edge of the Sahara Desert. Here, visitors can take camel rides, stay in desert camps, and admire unforgettable sunsets over the dunes. A full day in Merzouga allows for visits to Berber families, enjoying traditional music, and exploring the unique black desert.</p>

  <p>From the desert, the itinerary continues through the bustling market of Rissani, the spectacular Todra Gorge, and the beautiful Dades Valley. The road then leads to Ouarzazate, nicknamed the "Hollywood of Africa," and to the UNESCO World Heritage site of Ait Ben Haddou, a fortified village that has served as the backdrop for numerous films. After crossing the High Atlas, the tour finally arrives in Marrakech.</p>

  <p>The final full day is dedicated to discovering Marrakech''s must-see sites, including the Koutoubia Mosque, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahia_Palace" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong>Bahia Palace</strong></a>, the Saadian Tombs, the Majorelle Gardens, and the lively Jemaa el-Fnaa square. The adventure ends with an airport transfer on the last day.</p>'
