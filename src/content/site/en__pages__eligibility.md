Participation in Morocco Excursions programs requires a basic level of physical, emotional, and social readiness.

### **General Requirements**

Participants should:

- Be in good physical health and able to travel for extended periods
- Be comfortable in new **cultural** environments
- Be able to participate in group activities
- Have a positive and respectful attitude

### **Travel Conditions**

Our programs may include:

- Long driving distances
- Walking in uneven terrain (medinas, mountains, desert)
- Basic conditions in rural or desert areas

Participants should be prepared to adapt to these conditions.

### **Age Requirements**

- Most programs are open to participants aged 18+
- Younger participants may join with group supervision

We encourage participants to contact us in advance if they have specific needs so we can assess suitability and provide appropriate support.
