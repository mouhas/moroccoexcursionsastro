- title: 'Day 1, Evening: Merzouga - Camel Ride on the Dunes - Camp - Sunset'
  html: '<p>The camel ride in Merzouga begins around 4:00 PM in winter and around 6:00 PM in summer and during the warmer months. We will meet at our office in Merzouga, where you can park your car, use the restroom, and get ready for the excursion. You will ride camels across the dunes of Erg Chebbi, the highest and most famous in Morocco. These golden dunes are among the highest in North Africa, and their color changes beautifully throughout the day.</p>

    <p>As you head deeper into the desert, the guides will stop for photos and to let you enjoy the stunning views. One of the best moments is stopping atop a dune to watch the sunset. The sand takes on orange and red hues as the sun goes down, offering a truly magical spectacle.</p>

    <p>Upon arrival at the desert camp, you will be welcomed with a glass of mint tea. After settling into your tent, you can walk on the dunes, relax, or try sandboarding. In the evening, a delicious Moroccan dinner will be served in the restaurant tent, with dishes such as tagine and couscous.</p>

    <p>After dinner, the camp comes alive with the sound of Berber music. Local guides play drums around the campfire, and you can join in to play or dance. At night, the sky is filled with bright stars, perfect for stargazing and enjoying the calm of the Sahara.</p>'
