A camel ride across the magnificent Sahara is one of the most unforgettable experiences you can have while visiting Morocco. A camel trek takes you on a fantastic journey to discover the magic and silence of the [**Sahara**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahara).

Camel trekking is one of the most popular tourist attractions in Morocco and a must while visiting the Sahara desert. You'll ride camels across a sea of sand, enjoy sunrise and sunset over the tallest dunes, and stay in a desert bivouac or with a nomadic family for one or several nights.

If you have enough time in the desert, it's possible to join a multi-day camel trek while staying at oases and wild camping deep in the desert. If you join a shorter camel ride, you can spend one or two nights in a wild, standard or luxury camp with a private bathroom.

While riding a camel across the dunes isn't the most comfortable mode of transport for everyone, it's without a doubt a "must" for any tourist who wants to discover the nomadic way of life. If you've ever wanted to be Lawrence of Arabia for a short while, this is the perfect chance to do it.

Camel trekking excursions are available in various places across Morocco, but the most popular is the Merzouga desert (the dunes of Erg Chebbi), which are the tallest dunes in Morocco and also known as the golden dunes due to their colour.

### Desert Treks & Camel Rides

Without a doubt, spending a night in the Sahara desert should be on your wish list for a **trip to Morocco**. Cross the Sahara's sand dunes on a camel, watch the sun set over the dunes, and sleep in a desert camp under a million stars. A life experience not to be missed.

At Morocco Excursions, we organize several desert tours across Morocco and offer a variety of camping options. We have luxury bivouacs with private bathrooms in every tent, and a luxury camp with heating and air conditioning. The luxury camps are fully equipped with everything needed to provide a high-quality service and a comfortable stay.

We also have standard bivouacs available for budget travellers, students and more. Every bivouac has private tents with shared bathrooms and is well stocked with beds, blankets, sheets and everything you'll need for a comfortable stay. Each tent is traditionally designed to give you a nomadic experience.

If you're not interested in riding a camel, all camps are also reachable by ATV (quad bike), buggy or 4×4.

Camel trek and night in the desert
If you're looking for a real Sahara experience and want to live like a nomad for a few days, we recommend joining one of our camel treks with wild camping. You'll ride a camel deep into the dunes, away from other camps and other tourists. There will be food, water, small camping tents, sleeping bags and a medium-sized tent that serves as a kitchen and restaurant. The excursion can last from one to five days, depending on the client's request.

For a different and unique experience, we also recommend staying with a local nomadic family. You'll cross the dunes by camel to reach the other side of the desert, where nomadic families live. You'll discover their way of life and spend the night in their tent or earthen home.
