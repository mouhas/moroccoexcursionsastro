notes:
- This exciting 8-day journey takes you through Morocco’s most beautiful places, starting in Casablanca and ending in Marrakech. You’ll visit historic cities like Rabat and Fes, see amazing desert landscapes in Merzouga, and explore stunning valleys and mountains along the way. The entire trip spans approximately 1,500 kilometers (930 miles) of diverse scenery.
- Packing the right items will make your trip more comfortable. Don’t forget a power adapter (Type C or E works in Morocco) for your electronics. The sun can be strong, so be sure to bring sunscreen and a reusable water bottle to stay hydrated. For the desert portion, pack sunglasses to protect your eyes from the bright sand, a scarf to cover your face during windy moments, and a small flashlight for moving around the camp at night. It’s also smart to bring hand sanitizer and basic medicines for stomach issues, just in case.
- This popular tour fills up quickly during busy travel seasons, so it’s best to book your spot early. The most pleasant times to visit are spring (March to May) and fall (September to November) when temperatures are comfortable. While the tour operates year-round, summer months can get extremely hot in the desert areas – sometimes over 40°C (104°F). If you travel in summer, be prepared for intense heat during the day.
- You’ll need a moderate level of fitness for this adventure. The old city areas (medinas) have uneven stone streets with steps and slopes that require careful walking. The camel ride to the desert camp lasts about an hour, but if you prefer not to ride, there’s always the option to take a 4×4 vehicle instead. Most days involve some walking at historical sites, and <a href="/tours/ait-ben-haddou-day-trip-from-marrakech" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong>Kasbah Ait Ben Haddou</strong></a>, so comfortable shoes are a must.
