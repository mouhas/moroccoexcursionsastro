- title: 'Day 2: A day to visit Marrakech'
  html: '<p>After meeting your guide, you will spend the entire day exploring the beautiful city of Marrakech. Your day will begin with a visit to some of the city’s most famous landmarks. First, you will see the impressive Koutoubia Mosque, known for its tall, beautiful minaret. Next, you will visit the Saadian tombs, where members of the Saadian dynasty are buried, showcasing stunning architecture and history.</p>

    <p>After that, you will explore the elegant Bahia Palace, famous for its intricate decorations and peaceful gardens. Then, you will walk through the historic Jewish quarter called the "Mellah," where you can learn about the rich cultural history of Marrakech. You will also visit the Menara Gardens, a calm and green space with a large reflecting pool and beautiful views of the Atlas Mountains.</p>

    <p>Later, you will enjoy a peaceful stroll in the colorful Majorelle Gardens, known for their vibrant blue colors and exotic plants. After visiting these famous spots, your guide will take you into the heart of the Medina of Marrakech. Here, you will experience the lively Jemaa el-Fnaa square, a busy marketplace full of energy, where storytellers, musicians, dancers, and jugglers gather to entertain visitors.</p>

    <p>As the day winds down, you will have some free time to relax. You can choose to watch the beautiful sunset either from your hotel or riad, or from the terrace of a nearby local restaurant that offers a fantastic view of Jemaa el-Fnaa square below. This will be the perfect way to end a full day of discovery and new experiences in Marrakech.</p>

    <p><strong>Accommodation:</strong> <br/><em>Standard</em>: Hotel Riad Marana or similar<br/><em>Superior</em>: Hotel Riad Bahia Salam or similar</p>'
- title: 'Day 3: Marrakech City - Atlas Mountains - Ait Ben Haddou - Skoura - Roses Valley - Dades Valley'
  html: '<p>Today, we will start traveling south, crossing the High Atlas Mountains. Along the way, we will stop several times to enjoy the stunning views of the mountains and the famous Tizi N’tichka pass, which is 2,260 meters high. This is one of the most beautiful mountain passes in Morocco.</p>

    <p>Next, we will drive through small Berber villages and the peaceful Ounila Valley. Our destination is Ait Benhaddou Kasbah, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. This kasbah is one of the most impressive red-earth castles found along the old Kasbah route.</p>

    <p>Ait Benhaddou was an important trading center in the 17th century. It has also been featured in many famous movies like <em>Gladiator</em>, <em>Lawrence of Arabia</em>, and the TV series <em>Game of Thrones</em>. After we arrive, we will have lunch and take time to explore the kasbah, learning about its history and architecture.</p>

    <p>After visiting Ait Benhaddou, we will continue our journey through the beautiful palm groves of Skoura and then pass through the famous Valley of Roses, known for its colorful rose gardens and fragrant air.</p>

    <p>In the evening, we will arrive in the Dades Valley, a breathtaking and peaceful area surrounded by impressive rock formations and cliffs. Here, we will spend the night and enjoy the calm atmosphere of this wonderful valley.</p>

    <p><strong>Accommodation:</strong> <br/><em>Standard</em>: Hotel Kasbah Didis or similar<br/><em>Superior</em>: Hotel Ksar Sultan or similar</p>'
- title: 'Day 4: Dades Valley - Tinghir - Todra Gorges - Erfoud - Merzouga Desert'
  html: '<p>On the third day, we will travel from Dades to the Sahara Desert. Along the way, we will drive through many small villages and beautiful valleys. Our first stop will be at the Oasis of Tinghir, a peaceful green area surrounded by the desert. After that, we will continue to Todra Gorge, a stunning natural site made of tall limestone cliffs carved by a river. We will spend some time here to explore and enjoy the amazing views of the gorge.</p>

    <p>Next, our journey will take us through several towns and cities, including Tinjdad, Melaab, Erfoud, and the large Saharan oasis called Tafilalet. This area is rich in history and culture, and you will see the changing landscapes as we get closer to the desert.</p>

    <p>When we reach Merzouga, the gateway to the Sahara, your schedule will depend on the time of day. If you arrive early, you may have a short rest before the next activity. Otherwise, you will begin the camel ride right away. During the summer months, the camel trek starts around 5:30 PM, while in the winter it usually begins between 3:00 and 4:00 PM.</p>

    <p>You will enjoy about one hour riding a camel across the dunes, watching the sun set, and painting the sky with beautiful colors. After the ride, you will arrive at the desert camp where you will be welcomed with a warm cup of traditional tea. You can check into your comfortable tent and then have some free time to explore the dunes around the camp or try sandboarding on the soft sand.</p>

    <p>In the evening, local guides will play traditional drum music around a bonfire. This is a wonderful chance to listen, dance, and experience the magic of the desert night under the clear sky filled with stars. Please note, the bonfire and drum performances usually happen during the cooler months and only when the weather is good, since hot temperatures and desert storms may prevent the fire from being lit.</p>

    <p><strong>Accommodation:</strong> <br/><em>Standard</em>: Mouhou Standard Desert Camp or similar<br/><em>Superior</em>: Mouhou Luxury Desert Camp or similar</p>'
- title: 'Day 5: Merzouga - Berber Families - Old Villages - Mines of Fossiles - Erg Chebbi Desert'
  html: '<p>After breakfast and a shower, we will start our desert adventure in Erg Chebbi by a 4WD car. You will travel through charming Berber villages, including Hassi Labied and Khamlia. At Hassi Labied, we will take a short break in a peaceful garden where you can see an ancient irrigation system made up of small canals that help water the plants. It’s a great place to learn about traditional desert farming methods.</p>

    <p>Next, we will continue our 4×4 journey toward Khamlia. On the way, you may meet local people who will show you their beautiful desert foxes, along with other unique animals and plants that only live in the Sahara Desert. When we arrive in Khamlia, you will discover its special history as a village settled by black people who came from Sudan many years ago. Here, a member of a local music group will warmly welcome you. You can enjoy a cup of tea while listening to their calming Gnawa music, which has a deep spiritual meaning.</p>

    <p>After leaving Khamlia, we will drive to a spot that offers a stunning view of the golden sand dunes stretching as far as the eye can see. Then, we will visit a nomadic family nearby. You will meet the family members, learn about their simple desert lifestyle, and share some of your own traditions with them while enjoying a delicious cup of tea together.</p>

    <p>Our journey will then take us to a high plateau where you can explore fascinating black volcanic rocks and discover ancient fossils hidden in the desert soil. We will stop here to enjoy a relaxing picnic lunch under the shade of a tamarisk tree, surrounded by the peaceful desert landscape.</p>

    <p>Finally, after exploring these amazing desert places, we will head back to your accommodation.</p>

- title: 'Day 6: Merzouga - Rissani - Alnif - Nkob - Agdz - Draa Valley - Ouarzazate City'
  html: '<p>On the fourth day, we will wake you up early so you can experience the beautiful sunrise in the desert. It is a magical moment when the sky changes colors and the desert comes alive with soft light. After enjoying this peaceful scene, you will have breakfast and take a refreshing shower to get ready for the day ahead.</p>

    <p>Once we are ready, we will start our journey towards Ouarzazate by taking the southern road. Along the way, you will see many amazing landscapes. The road passes through a wide, black rocky desert that stretches as far as the eye can see. We will travel through several small towns and villages, including Rissani, Alnif, Tazarine, and Nkoub. Each place has its own unique charm and traditional atmosphere.</p>

    <p>Next, we will reach Agdez, a peaceful town where the desert meets the beautiful Draa Valley. The valley is full of green palm trees, ancient kasbahs (old fortified homes), and small farms that contrast beautifully with the surrounding desert. You can enjoy the calm and quiet of this lovely area as we drive through it.</p>

    <p>Depending on how many stops you want to make along the way to take photos, explore, or rest, we will arrive in Ouarzazate in the afternoon or early evening. Ouarzazate is known as the “Hollywood of Africa” because many famous movies have been filmed here. It is a great place to relax after a long day of travel and to prepare for the next part of your adventure.</p>
