overviewHtml: '<p>Join us on an unforgettable 10-day tour across Morocco, starting from the vibrant city of <a href="https://morocco-excursion.com/marrakech-tours/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><b>Marrakech</b></a>. This journey will take you through Morocco’s most beautiful and famous places, including the <strong>Imperial Cities</strong>, the <strong>Atlas Mountains</strong>, and the <strong>Sahara Desert</strong>.</p>

  <p>We begin in <strong>Marrakech</strong>, a lively and colorful city known for its ancient palaces, busy souks, and rich culture. From there, we drive through the breathtaking <strong>High Atlas Mountains</strong>, passing through traditional Berber villages and scenic valleys. Along the way, you’ll see historic <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasbah" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong>kasbahs</strong></a> (fortified villages) and meet local <strong>nomad families</strong> who live in the mountains and desert areas.</p>

  <p>One of the highlights is the visit to the impressive <strong>Todra Gorges</strong>, where tall cliffs rise on both sides of a river canyon — perfect for walking and taking photos. Then, continue to the edge of the <strong>Sahara Desert</strong>, where you’ll ride a camel across the golden <strong>Erg Chebbi dunes</strong> and spend the night in a <strong>desert camp under the stars</strong>, enjoying traditional Berber music and food.</p>

  <p>The tour also takes you to the historic city of <strong>Fes</strong>, known for its ancient medina and traditional craftsmanship. Next, explore <strong>Meknes</strong>, another imperial city, and walk among the ruins of the <strong>Roman city of Volubilis</strong>, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.</p>

  <p>From there, we travel to the north of Morocco to visit the beautiful town of <strong>Chefchaouen</strong>, famous for its peaceful atmosphere and blue-painted streets, set high in the <strong>Rif Mountains</strong>. Afterward, we head to <strong>Rabat</strong>, Morocco’s capital city, where you’ll visit royal landmarks and coastal views, followed by a stop in <strong>Casablanca</strong> to see the impressive <strong>Hassan II Mosque</strong> by the sea.</p>

  <p>The tour ends back in <strong>Marrakech</strong>, where you’ll have time to relax, shop, or enjoy the city one more time before departure.</p>'

notes:
- Some days involve 6–8 hours of driving, especially between Marrakech–Dades, Merzouga–<strong><a href="https://morocco-excursion.com/fes-tours/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Fes</a></strong>, and Chefchaouen–Rabat. The scenery is beautiful, with stops along the way, but bring snacks, water, and motion sickness medicine if needed.
- Bring light clothing for the day and warm layers for the desert night. In the Merzouga desert, days are hot (up to 30°C), but nights can be cool or even cold in October.
- It’s respectful to wear shoulder-covering tops and long pants or skirts, especially when visiting mosques or medinas.
- Exchange or withdraw money in major cities like Marrakech, Fes, or Casablanca. While cities accept cards, rural areas, markets, and small vendors may only take cash (dirhams).
- Cities and hotels usually have Wi-Fi, but the connection is weak in the mountains and in the desert.
